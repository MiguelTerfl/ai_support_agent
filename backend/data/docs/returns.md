# Returns and refunds policy

## Return window
You can return any product within 30 calendar days after the delivery date. After that period we no longer accept returns for change of mind, although the warranty for manufacturing defects remains in force (see the warranty policy).

## Product condition
The product must be unused, in its original packaging and with all accessories, manuals and tags. If the packaging was damaged in transit, let us know within 48 hours after delivery and attach photos.

## Products that cannot be returned
We do not accept returns of customized products, of in-ear headphones whose hygiene seal is broken, or of final-clearance items marked "final sale".

## Return shipping cost
Return shipping is paid by the customer, except when the product arrived defective, damaged or different from what you ordered. In those cases Aurora Electro pays for the return label.

## How to request a return
Go to My account, open Orders and choose Request return on the corresponding order. You can also ask the assistant to open a ticket with the order number and the reason. We will email you a prepaid label or shipping instructions.

## Refund
Once we receive and inspect the product, the refund is made to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Cash payments at OXXO and SPEI bank transfers are refunded by bank transfer to the account you give us.
