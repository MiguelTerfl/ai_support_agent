# Shipping and delivery

## Shipping cost
Shipping is free on purchases of $999 MXN or more. On purchases under $999 MXN the shipping cost is $99 MXN.

## Delivery times
We deliver in 3 to 5 business days to Mexico City, Guadalajara and Monterrey and their metropolitan areas. In the rest of the country delivery takes 5 to 8 business days. Orders placed after 14:00 (Central time) or on a weekend are processed on the next business day.

## Carriers and tracking
We ship with Estafeta and DHL. When your order leaves our warehouse you receive the tracking number and tracking link by email. You can also check the status of your order with the assistant by giving your order number and the email you bought with.

## Delivery attempts
The carrier makes up to 2 delivery attempts. If nobody is there to receive the package, it is held at the carrier's branch for 5 business days before being returned to our warehouse.

## Restrictions
We do not deliver to PO boxes. If the address is wrong or incomplete, contact support before the order is shipped so we can correct it; once the order is in transit the address can no longer be changed.
