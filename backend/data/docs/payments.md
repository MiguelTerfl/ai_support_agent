# Payment methods and cancellations

## Accepted methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit and debit cards, cash payment at OXXO stores, and SPEI bank transfer. For your security, we will never ask for your full card number or your password by chat, email or phone.

## Validity of cash and SPEI payments
The OXXO payment reference is valid for 48 hours. The SPEI transfer reference is valid for 24 hours. If you do not pay within that time, the order is cancelled automatically and you can place it again.

## Interest-free installments
We offer 3, 6 and 12 interest-free monthly installments on purchases of $1,500 MXN or more with credit cards from participating banks. Purchases with a debit card, OXXO or SPEI do not qualify for interest-free installments.

## How the charge appears
On your statement the charge appears as AURORA ELECTRO. If you do not recognize a charge, first check your confirmation email and, if you are still unsure, open a ticket with the amount and the date.

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel at no cost while the order is in processing status, that is, before it leaves the warehouse. The refund for a cancellation is credited to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days. Once the order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled, but you can request a return when you receive it.
