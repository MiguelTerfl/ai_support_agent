# Warranty policy

## Warranty period
Small appliances (blenders, coffee makers, electric ovens, irons) have a 12-month warranty from the delivery date. Robot vacuums and the Pulse audio line have a 24-month warranty. Batteries and wear-and-tear accessories, such as filters and brushes, have a 6-month warranty.

## What it covers
The warranty covers manufacturing defects and malfunctions under normal use. If the product cannot be repaired, we replace it with the same or an equivalent one, or refund the amount paid.

## What it does not cover
Damage from impacts or drops, liquid spills on electrical equipment, use with a voltage other than the one indicated, repairs made by third parties, and normal wear from use are not covered.

## How to claim the warranty
Open a support ticket with the order number, the product's serial number (on the bottom or back label), a description of the fault and, if possible, a photo or short video. Our team will reply with shipping instructions.

## Repair time
Once we receive the product at our service center, the repair or replacement is completed within a maximum of 15 business days. Round-trip shipping for warranty service is free for you.
