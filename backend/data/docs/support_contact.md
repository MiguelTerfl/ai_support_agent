# Support, contact and privacy

## Support hours
The support team is available Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 18:00 (Mexico Central time). Outside those hours you can leave a ticket and we will handle it on the next business day. There is no service on official public holidays.

## Response time
The first response time for a ticket is 24 business hours. Tickets related to warranties or damaged products are prioritized.

## Channels
You can write to us through this assistant, open a ticket, or email soporte@aurora-electro.example. We have no physical stores; all sales and support are online, and we only sell and ship within Mexico.

## Account and password
To change your password, go to My account and choose Security. If you forgot your password, use the Recover password option on the sign-in screen; we will send a link to your registered email.

## Privacy and ARCO rights
You can request access, rectification, cancellation or opposition to the processing of your personal data (ARCO rights) by writing to privacidad@aurora-electro.example. We reply within a maximum of 20 business days. See our privacy notice in the site footer.
