# Electronic invoicing

## How to request your invoice
We issue CFDI 4.0 electronic invoices. Go to My account, open Orders and select Request invoice, or reply to your purchase confirmation email with your tax details.

## Information you need
Your RFC, your business name exactly as it appears on your tax status certificate (constancia de situación fiscal), your fiscal postal code, your tax regime and the applicable CFDI use. If any of these details does not match the tax authority's records (SAT), the invoice cannot be issued.

## Timing and deadline to request it
The invoice is sent to your email within 24 business hours after your request. You must request it within the same calendar month in which you made the purchase; once the month has closed, invoices for that period can no longer be issued or replaced.

## Corrections and cancellations
If you made a mistake in your details, you can ask for a replacement within the same calendar month. Invoices for orders with a return are cancelled or offset with a credit note, depending on the case.
